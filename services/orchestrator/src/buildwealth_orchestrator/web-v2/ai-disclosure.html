AI disclosure

How Copilot uses context

Copilot can help draft, explain, compare, and review financial information. It is a helper inside BuildWealth, not a decision maker.

Private beta draft. Provider-specific wording must be reviewed against each supported AI provider's terms and data-handling settings before public launch.

What Copilot Can Do

Copilot can summarize context, explain simulations, draft recommendations, prepare profile updates, compare options, and help you review assumptions.

Copilot output is a draft or suggestion. You can accept, reject, edit, or ignore it.

What May Be Sent to a Provider

If you configure an AI provider or use Copilot, BuildWealth may send the information needed to answer your request to that provider.

  • Conversation messages and instructions.
  • Financial profile details relevant to the request.
  • Portfolio, holding, transaction, benchmark, allocation, or risk context.
  • Simulation inputs and outputs.
  • Research notes, evidence packets, recommendations, and decision history.
  • Tool results needed to explain what BuildWealth found or changed.

Provider Keys

BuildWealth stores provider API keys as encrypted workspace secrets. Raw key values are not returned after saving. Removing a key stops BuildWealth from using that provider for the current workspace, but it does not close the provider account.

Limits

AI-generated content can be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or based on misunderstood context. BuildWealth does not guarantee Copilot output.

Use Copilot to understand options and prepare drafts. Do not treat Copilot output as financial, investment, tax, legal, accounting, or brokerage advice.

Plain-language rule: Copilot can help you think, but you decide. For major decisions, review the assumptions and talk with a qualified professional.
