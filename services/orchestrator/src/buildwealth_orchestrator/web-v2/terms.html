Rules for using BuildWealth

BuildWealth is software for organizing financial information, running simulations, reviewing tradeoffs, and keeping a record of assumptions. It does not make decisions for you.

Private beta draft. This page is implementation-aligned product wording and must be reviewed before public launch.

No Professional Advice

BuildWealth is a software tool. BuildWealth is not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, tax advisor, accountant, attorney, or financial planner.

BuildWealth does not place trades for you, manage money for you, or make decisions for you. Any simulation, score, review, draft, suggestion, or recommendation is informational and must be reviewed by you.

Simulations

Simulations are experiments. They use assumptions, estimates, and data available at the time. Actual outcomes can be different.

You should review the assumptions and update them as your life, market conditions, tax rules, and goals change.

AI Output

Copilot and other AI-assisted features can produce incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated information. AI output should be treated as a draft or starting point, not as a final decision.

Your Responsibility

You are responsible for the information you enter, the assumptions you choose, and the decisions you make. For important financial, investment, tax, legal, or estate decisions, consult a qualified professional.

Provider Accounts and Keys

If you add a provider API key, you confirm that you are allowed to use it with BuildWealth. You are responsible for the provider account, provider charges, limits, and provider terms.

If you connect a financial institution through Plaid, you authorize read-only retrieval of the accounts and investment data you select. BuildWealth does not provide trading or money-movement functionality through that connection. Plaid and the institution may apply their own terms and availability limits.

Approved connections may update automatically. When you disconnect, provider access is revoked and you choose whether BuildWealth keeps the last observation as a frozen, stale record or removes connected observations. Manual entries and CSV imports are separate and remain under your control.

Removing a key from BuildWealth does not close your provider account. Closing BuildWealth access does not delete your hosted identity-provider account.

Access Closure and Data Deletion

Close BuildWealth access disables your BuildWealth account, removes active workspace memberships, and signs you out. It does not delete workspace files, backups, encrypted provider secrets, audit records, or your hosted identity-provider account.

Delete BuildWealth data is a separate destructive flow with preview, confirmation, a recovery window, and final purge by a private maintenance job after the recovery window ends.

Requesting deletion immediately begins revocation of connected financial institutions. Canceling local deletion during the recovery window does not restore a provider connection that was already revoked.

Service Changes and Availability

BuildWealth is in private beta. Features, providers, data structures, simulations, and hosted operations may change as the product matures.
