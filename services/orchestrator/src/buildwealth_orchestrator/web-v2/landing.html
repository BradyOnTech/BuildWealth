The capabilities

One workspace, from holdings to horizon.

Six native capabilities that own their data, their calculations, and their audit trail — not dashboards bolted onto someone else's tools.

01

Portfolio Analysis

Performance · Attribution · Risk

Explains performance, benchmarks, attribution, allocation, and risk — and models the impact of a trade before you make it. See not just what you hold, but why it moved.

02

Plan & Simulations

Projections · Monte Carlo · Backtests

Run projections, compare scenarios, and stress-test with Monte Carlo and historical backtests. A plain-language Plan Strength label tells you how reliably the plan funds what matters — before the metrics.

03

Import Workbench

Preview · Reconcile · Audit

Preview, reconcile, and apply imported data — with a durable Import Report that explains exactly how each file changed your portfolio. Nothing becomes state until you say so.

04

Asset Registry

Resolve · Classify · Price

Resolves, classifies, enriches, and prices every investable asset your portfolio, plan, and research depend on — and flags the ambiguous ones for review before they skew a number.

05

Copilot, grounded

Your data · Traceable · Drafts

Ask questions in plain language and get answers built only from your canonical state, each one carrying the context it used. Copilot proposes; you review and decide.

06

Context Intelligence

Evidence · Conflicts · Trail

Captures, selects, and audits the financial context behind every recommendation — surfacing conflicts to resolve and leaving a context trace you can inspect end to end.

Questions

The things worth knowing first.

Is my financial data private?
Yes. BuildWealth is local-first: your data lives in an isolated workspace with its own store, not a row in a shared multi-tenant table. Hosted sessions keep the same isolation, with tenant-scoped backups you control.
Does BuildWealth give investment advice?
No. BuildWealth helps you organize, model, and understand your finances. It is not a registered investment adviser and does not provide personalized investment, tax, or legal advice — the decisions are yours.
What can I track and plan with BuildWealth?
Track portfolio performance, allocation, attribution, and risk; model long-horizon plans with projections, scenarios, Monte Carlo simulations, and historical backtests; and read a plain-language Plan Strength label that tells you how reliably the plan holds.
Where does the copilot get its answers?
Only from your own canonical data. Every answer carries a traceable context trail, and copilot suggestions arrive as drafts you review before anything changes.
How do I get my data into BuildWealth?
Through the Import Workbench: preview and reconcile statements and holdings, then apply them with a durable audit report. Nothing becomes portfolio state until you approve it.
